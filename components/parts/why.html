10 Problem cards

Three cards, one problem each.

A clinic asked its patients what goes wrong when they book a visit. Each card holds one answer, and the callout sums them up.

The queue is long

You wait two hours in a full room, and you might still not be seen today.

The phone is always busy

You call ten times before anyone picks up, and then the slot is gone.

Nobody reminds you

You forget the visit, and lose your place for weeks.

In one sentence

Book a visit in one minute, wait at home, and get a reminder the day before.
