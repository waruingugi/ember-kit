Shop help

Orders inside Nairobi arrive in one to two working days. Orders to other towns take three to five working days. We text you a tracking link as soon as your parcel leaves our shop.

Yes. You have 14 days from the day it arrives to send an item back, as long as it is unused and in its box. We refund the money to your PocketPay within three days of getting it.

We deliver to Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret and most other towns in Kenya. Delivery costs KSh 350 in Nairobi and KSh 600 anywhere else. Orders over KSh 5,000 ship free.

At checkout, choose PocketPay and type your phone number. You get a prompt on your phone: enter your PIN to pay. Your order is confirmed in a few seconds.

You can change the size, colour or address until we pack it, which is usually within two hours. Open your order and press Edit. After that, call us on 0700 123 456 and we will try to help.
