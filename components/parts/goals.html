11 Goal list

Four goals, each with a test.

An online shop's goals for its checkout. Each one comes with a simple test, so the team knows if it kept it.

  1. 1

    Fast

    The checkout page opens in under one second.

    How we'll knowPage open < 1 s
  2. 2

    Clear

    The total you see first is the total you pay.

    How we'll knowNo surprise fees, ever
  3. 3

    Simple

    Pay in three taps, with PocketPay or a card.

    How we'll knowPaid in under 1 minute
  4. 4

    Calm

    One email per order. No spam after you buy.

    How we'll knowAt most 1 email per order
