14 Flow

A photo's trip, in four steps.

When you post a photo, it passes through four steps. The arrows draw in, then a dot walks the steps in order.

1

Pick

You pick a photo and press Post.

2

Upload

Your phone sends the photo to the server.

3

Shrink

Helpers make small copies for slow phones.

4

Show

Your friends see it in their feed.

Hand-drawnThe arrows draw in one by one, then a dot walks the four steps in order.
