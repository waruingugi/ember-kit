Compare Sticky pages

Four ways to stack steps as you scroll.

Each option shows the same four steps: setting up payouts for a shop on PocketPay. Scroll the page and all four move together. Then pick one, mix two, or keep sticky pages out.

Why these were removed. On 29 October you took stacked pages out because they kept breaking. Our guess at the cause: the old pages scrolled inside a small box of their own, and a box that scrolls inside a page is easy to get stuck in, mostly on phones. So none of these four has a box of its own. They all use the page's own scroll.

A

Full cover, as easyui made it

Every page sticks at the same line, so the next page covers the last one whole. You only ever see one step.

easyui: Sticky Pages

Try: scroll slowly and watch each page slide over the one before.

Pick one, or mix two.

  • A if each step should have the whole space to itself.
  • B if seeing the pile (how far you are) matters most.
  • C if you want B with a little depth as each page is covered.
  • D if you would rather not bring sticking pages back at all.
  • Or say a mix, like "B, with A's rounded top", or none.

The winner is rebuilt as a house part, checked on phones first, and written in the choices log.
