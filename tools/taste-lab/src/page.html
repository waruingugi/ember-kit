00 Taste Lab · Round 1

Pick what feels right. Claude learns your taste.

Here are {{COUNT}} parts that every product needs. Each one comes in four to six working versions, {{OPTS}} in all. They all use the same sample data, so only the design is different. Try them, then pick one of each.

  1. Try every version. Menus, pop-ups and messages open inside their own card, so you can compare them side by side.
  2. Pick one per part, or press "None of these". Add a note like "B, but slower".
  3. Done. Picks save to this page. Claude reads them, writes down what you lean towards, and builds the winners into the kit.
A

Plain. How shadcn/ui builds it, in our look. Familiar keys, quiet motion.

B

Lively. Motion is the point, from free beUI and easyui parts.

C

Mix. The best of A and B, plus our motion skills, depth and thinking orbs.

D

Reference, pushed. Your current house part, one step further.

E

Off-look. Steps outside the ember look on purpose. Marked with a stripe.

F

Wild card. A bold idea, shown only where it helps.

Each card lists where its idea came from, what it needs, how the keys work, what happens with motion off, and which UX law it bends (Fitts, Hick, Jakob or proximity). The small tags under it are what Claude counts.
