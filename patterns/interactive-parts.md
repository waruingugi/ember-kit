# Interactive parts

> Part of the design system. Read `~/.claude/design/DESIGN.md` first; it says when to read this file.

**What it is.** 29 parts a reader works with: navigation, forms, tables, status,
cards and moving surfaces. Each one started as a React component the owner shared, and
was rebuilt in plain HTML in our look on 2026-10-29. The owner reviewed them the same
day. Each one is a file in `components/parts/`, named by its number: `c03.html` to
`c31.html` (`c01`/`c02` are the line charts). All of them are also in the showcase,
`components/showcase/index.html`, which is the master copy.

**How to use it.** Find the part in the table below, then build the page with
`node scripts/assemble.mjs <ids> -o page.html` (how: `components/parts/README.md`).
That brings its CSS, markup and script in the showcase's order, so it looks exactly
the same. Don't copy pieces by hand and don't restyle a part for one page. If it
needs to change, change it in the showcase, then run `node scripts/split.mjs`.

## Part specs

Enough to rebuild each Taste Lab part (`n29`–`n51`, and `n07`, rebuilt from round 3) without its code. Every one sits in `.frame > .fbody`, usually with a `.stage` inside, and most have a `.note` (`aria-live="polite"`) under the stage that says in words what just happened; where a spec names another live line, that one replaces the note. The heading (`.kit-h`) and the try line (`.kit-p`) are free copy: write them for the page. Springs are the showcase tokens (`--sp-snappy` .37 s, `--sp-bouncy` .49 s, `--sp-soft` .67 s, each with `-d` for its length). The orange tick is one SVG used by many parts: `<svg class="tk" viewBox="0 0 22 22"><circle cx="11" cy="11" r="11"/><path d="M6.6 11.4 9.6 14.3l5.8-6.4"/></svg>`, 20 px, the circle `--accent` popping from `scale(.6)` over .3 s bouncy, the white 2 px path drawing in (`stroke-dasharray: 16`) over .34 s after .1 s. "Motion off" means `root.dataset.motion === "off"` or reduced motion: the same thing happens, without movement. With motion off the showcase turns off **every** animation and transition inside `.kit` (`!important`), so a part's resting CSS must already show its end state; one shared rule shows the tick's path (`html[data-motion="off"] .kit .tk path { stroke-dashoffset: 0 !important }`). Number text uses `var(--font-num)`; "big shadow" is `--shadow-lg`, "small shadow" `--shadow-sm`.

**n29 Pay button that rolls.** In a centred stage: `.b.pri` "Pay KSh 4,200" (min 172 px wide) and a plain `.b` "Save draft". The button is a one-cell grid holding three stacked states: the label, three 6 px dots (bouncing in turn, .12 s apart, .9 s loop) and a check icon with "Paid" (stroke 3, round ends and corner set on the `i-check` path itself, so every browser draws it soft; at 1.6 it looks sharp). The state in goes from `translateY(14px) scale(.9)` to none, the state out to `translateY(-14px) scale(.9)`, opacity .18 s, transform on bouncy. Press shrinks it to `scale(.94)` in .1 s. Timing: Paying 1.4 s → Paid for 2.2 s → back to the label; `aria-busy` while paying. The note: "Nothing paid yet." → "Paying…" → "KSh 4,200 paid to Duka Fresh."; draft says "Draft saved at HH:MM.".

**n30 Field that shakes and ticks.** A `novalidate` form, max 360 px, centred, 14 px gaps: Email and Password, each a 13.5 px weight-500 label, the `.in` with room on the right (40 px) for the tick, and a 13 px muted help line under it ("We send receipts here.", "8 or more characters."). Errors only show after the field is left with something in it, or after a submit. An error replaces the help line in `--accent-text` and sets `aria-invalid`: "Enter your email." / "Enter an email like amina@example.com." / "Enter a password." / "Use 8 or more characters. You have N.". Leaving a right field pops the tick inside its right edge. Submit with errors: each bad field's wrapper shakes (.42 s) and the first bad one gets focus. All right: a tick and "Account made for …" under the button "Create account" (left-aligned).

**n31 Spring switches.** A card, max 380 px, centred: title "Payment alerts" (22 px display font), then 48 px rows, label left (15 px, clicking it flips too), switch right: "Email me each receipt" (on), "SMS when a payment lands" (off), "Weekly summary" (on). Then, above a top line, a 20 px round-cornered (6 px) checkbox "Also send them to my accountant". Switch: `button role="switch"` 40×24, track `rgb(var(--ink-rgb)/.2)`, orange when on; 18 px knob (`--paper-raised`, small shadow) moves 16 px on bouncy; while pressed it squeezes to `scale(.85)` in .12 s. Hit area grows with a `::before`. Checkbox: orange when ticked, white path draws in .3 s, press `scale(.85)`. The note counts: "2 alerts on." / "1 alert on, copied to your accountant.".

**n32 Springy dropdown.** Stage min 320 px, items at the top. A row card (max 360 px, 12 px corners, small shadow): "Invoice #4821" bold over "Amina Otieno · KSh 4,200" muted, and a 40 px quiet `⋯` button (`aria-haspopup="menu"`, label "Actions for invoice 4821"). The menu (220 px, 5 px padding, 12 px corners, big shadow) opens below the button's right edge from `scale(.6)` at the top right corner, on bouncy; closes in .15 s. Items, 36 px tall (44 on touch), 15 px line icon + words + a mono letter key on the right: Edit invoice E, Duplicate D, Download PDF P, Send reminder R, a line, Delete invoice (orange text, no key). One shaded highlight (`rgb(var(--ink-rgb)/.07)`, 8 px corners) glides to the active row on snappy, following the pointer and the arrow keys. Picking an item writes its message in the note ("Copied as invoice #4822.").

**n33 Bloom menu.** Stage min 240 px, items at the bottom. A 320×214 box; the trigger is a 48 px round `⋯` `.b`, 14 px from the bottom, centred. ## Shared pieces (copy these with any part)

- **Helpers** at the top of the part-two script (the older `c` and `n01`–`n28` parts only; the Taste Lab parts carry their own small helpers, see `tools/taste-lab/README.md`, *Make a winner a house part*):
  - `spd()`: 0 when motion is off, 0.5 on calm, 1 on lively. Multiply distances and times by it.
  - `ease()`: the house curve, for script-driven motion.
  - `copy()`: clipboard with a fallback.
  - `onView()`: runs once at 40% visible.
  - `outside()`: a click outside a box. It ignores nodes the click itself removed.
- **`tabs()`**: the sliding marker for any row of choices (05, 06, 07, 17, 22 and the bar chart). It is Emil Kowalski's clipped-copy recipe. `follow(i, f)` drives it from scroll.
- **House checkbox `.chk`**: a custom box with a white round-cornered tick (and a round dash for "some"), ink when ticked. Never the browser's own box.
- **Small parts**:
  - `.b` buttons, with `.pri`, `.sm`, `.q` and `.danger` versions.
  - `.ib` icon buttons, `.in` inputs.
  - `.tag`, `.live` (the live dot), `.st` (status word with its mark).
  - `.kv` (label and value rows), `.pop` (popover box).
- **Icons**: one hidden SVG sprite (`#i-…` symbols, Lucide-style, 1.6 stroke, round caps) used with `<use href="#i-name">`. Logos (GitHub, Google, LinkedIn, X) are filled.

## Rules these parts follow

- **Progress is orange** (DESIGN.md, *Colours*): done steps are filled orange with a white round tick, and so is the line between steps.
- **Loops** only for the live dot or a busy sign while work is really running (the dot round the clock, the pulse on a running step). Things that looped for show in React became pointer- or scroll-driven: the dot field, the moving grid, the edge light, and card swap's autoplay.
- **Showcase them out in the open** (DESIGN.md, *Recurring parts*): copy the part's card straight onto the page. Keep the card (the inner wall); drop the outer wrapper and the file-name bar. Check that popups, sticky pages and hover cards still work there.
- **No glow, no green or red.** The white glare became a faint orange tint. Errors use orange text. Springs: the older parts settle without overshooting; the Taste Lab parts (`n29`–`n48`) overshoot a little, which is now the default for parts the reader works (DESIGN.md, *Motion*).
- **Heights glide.** When a box opens, closes or changes size, its height glides to the new size, padding included, and its content sits at the top (DESIGN.md, *Closing boxes glide all the way*). Some older parts still snap their height; follow the glide when you build anything new.
- **Downloads and outside images are blocked in artifacts.** Use Copy instead of Download, and drawings instead of photos.
- **Complete at rest.** With motion off every part shows its final state and still works.
- **Test at the default motion level, with a real hover.** Two bugs in the owner's review came from parts that only moved on lively, or only after a 4 s wait.

### Stacked pages: which kind, when

The owner keeps all four and lets Claude pick case by case (2026-11-01, `notes/choices.md`). Parts: `n26`, `n27`, `n28` and `c31`; the first compare page is `components/compare/sticky-pages.html`.

| Kind | Pick it when | Not when |
|---|---|---|
| `n27` **B · The deck** (the default) | 3 to 5 steps where seeing how far you are matters: setup, onboarding, "how it works" | Steps with a lot of text; more than 5 steps |
| `n26` **A · Full cover** | Few steps, and each step is a big screen of its own with a picture | Readers need to see the earlier steps |
| `n28` **C · The deck that settles** | The stack is the main picture of a section on a lively explainer page. At most one per page | Plain or calm pages; build guides |
| `c31` **D · Card swap** | More than 5 items, things to flip through (photos, cards), or a narrow space | Steps that must be read in order with their numbers in sight |

Rules for every kind:
- Never put the stack inside a box with its own scroll bar. That was the old bug.
- Never in build guides: they never pin, and a stack acts like a pin.
- Each page is at most about 60% of the screen height, so the next page shows up soon on phones.
- With motion `off` they still work: A and B are plain CSS, C turns into B, and D swaps cards with no slide.
- If none fits, use the stepper (10) or the timeline (24).
- `singles.mjs` cannot check a sticky part alone: sticky boxes sit at other heights when the part starts the page, so `n26`, `n27`, `n28` and `c31` show box moves only. Check them by scrolling the showcase at 1440 and 390 px instead.
